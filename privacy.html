Privacy Policy

Science By Hugs collects only what it needs to verify researchers, fill orders and deliver them, and it does not sell personal information. This policy covers sciencebyhugs.com, nexus.sciencebyhugs.com and the nexus app.

What we collect

TypeExamplesWhy
AccountName, email, phone, password (stored hashed)Sign-in and support
VerificationInstitution, role, research documents you uploadConfirm research status before any sale
OrdersItems, totals, delivery address, delivery notesFill and deliver orders, keep tax records
PaymentsPayment status and transaction IDMatch payments to orders. Card and bank details stay with PayPal, Zelle, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
MessagesTexts and emails with usAnswer questions and send order updates
DeviceIP address, browser, pages visited, cookiesSecurity, fraud prevention, keeping you signed in

Who we share it with

Only service providers that run the business for us (hosting and database, payment processors, email and text delivery), and authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Cookies

We use cookies needed for sign-in and security. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy for the full list.

How long we keep it

Account data while your account is open; order and payment records for 7 years for tax purposes; verification documents until your account closes plus 1 year.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to staff who need it, and passwords are never stored in plain text. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you if a breach affects your data, as Nevada law (NRS 603A) requires.

Your choices

You can view, correct or delete your account information, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing support@sciencebyhugs.com. Some order records must be kept for tax law even after deletion.

Nevada residents

We do not sell covered information as defined in NRS 603A. You may still submit a sale opt-out request to legal@sciencebyhugs.com and we will confirm it within 60 days.

Age

Our services are for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new effective date, and email account holders about material changes.
