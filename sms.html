Text Messaging (SMS) Terms

Science By Hugs texts you only about your account and orders, and only after you opt in.

  • Program: Science By Hugs order and account updates from (725) 310-7502.
  • What you get: order confirmations, drop-off windows, verification status and replies to your questions.
  • Frequency: varies with your orders, usually a few messages per order.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email support@sciencebyhugs.com.
  • Consent: opting in is not a condition of purchase.
  • Privacy: we do not share or sell your mobile number or text consent with third parties for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Carriers: carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
