Research Use Only & Researcher Verification

All Science By Hugs products are sold strictly for in-vitro and laboratory research, and only to buyers we have verified by hand.

What research use only means

  • Products are not drugs, supplements, food or cosmetics, and are not approved by the FDA.
  • They are not for human or animal use, and are not meant to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.
  • We do not give dosing, administration or medical advice, and staff will not answer those questions.

How verification works

  1. Create an account with your legal name, email and phone.
  2. Upload proof of research status: an institutional email or ID, a lab or company affiliation, or other documentation we accept.
  3. Agree to the research-use statement.
  4. We review every request by hand and approve, ask for more, or decline.

When we decline or cancel

We will decline or close an account if a request mentions personal use, dosing or bodybuilding; if documents cannot be confirmed; or if products appear to be resold for human use. Any open orders are cancelled and refunded.

Records

We keep verification records for as long as the account is open plus 1 year, and may re-verify accounts at any time.
